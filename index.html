GETTING THERE

The venue is located in Corte Franca, in the province of Brescia, near Lake Iseo and about a 30-minute drive from the city of Brescia.

The closest airports are Milano Linate, Bergamo Orio al Serio and Verona Valerio Catullo.

We recommend staying in the city of Brescia at one of the hotels we've selected (see below), as shuttle buses will run between the city centre and the wedding venue on the day of the wedding.

ACCOMMODATION
